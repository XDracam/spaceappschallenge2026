## Game, fonts and prototype assets

[Full third-party notices](game/public/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt) include Preact's
MIT notice, PeerJS's and its bundled dependencies' MIT/BSD-3-Clause notices, all
three fonts' copyrights and SIL OFL 1.1 terms, the existing Lucide notice,
build-tool credits and caniuse-lite attribution/license terms. Vite copies
this file to `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt` at the deployed site's root. `LICENSE.txt`
contains the project's Apache-2.0 terms. The game self-hosts unmodified Exo 2 (600/700) and Inter (400/500/600) from the exact Google Fonts stylesheet previously requested by the game. Source URLs, retrieval date and hashes are in [font sources](game/public/fonts/sources.json); [Exo 2](game/public/fonts/Exo-2-OFL.txt) and [Inter](game/public/fonts/Inter-OFL.txt) retain SIL OFL 1.1. IBM Plex Mono remains externally loaded by historical mockups. Lucide and IBM Plex Mono are used in the earlier
procurement mockup, rather than the integrated game's runtime.

AI-generated images: the Communications character portraits
(`game/public/portraits/*.webp`, 15 files) and the title-screen artwork
(`game/src/shell/assets/title-earth.webp`) were generated by the team with ChatGPT
(OpenAI) image generation in November 2026, then cropped and resized for the game.
The portraits depict fictional people. Each image carries a visible "AI-generated"
watermark and embedded metadata (EXIF ImageDescription and IPTC DigitalSourceType
`trainedAlgorithmicMedia` in XMP). Any further AI-generated image must be
watermarked the same way before it is added. Their use is subject to
[OpenAI's Terms of Use](https://openai.com/policies/terms-of-use/); AI-generated
images may not be eligible for copyright, so the project's Apache-2.0 license
applies to them only to the extent the project holds rights in them. The
procurement JPEGs under `procurement/` are browser screenshots of project
interfaces, not AI-generated.

## Submission narration and sharing assets

The current published trailer uses Microsoft neural text-to-speech (en-US-ChristopherNeural, via edge-tts), as recorded in its [credits](game/public/submission/credits.txt). Earlier retained exports in `video/` use Voicebox with Supertonic 3 (M2 in the archived T-Minus trailer; F1 in the superseded Mission Control draft). [Narration provenance](video/NARRATION.md) distinguishes the model's OpenRAIL-M terms from the sample-code MIT license and records what generation settings remain unknown. No model weights are shipped.

`game/public/social-title.webp` is an unchanged byte-for-byte copy of the labelled title artwork above. `game/public/favicon.svg` is an original neutral orbit icon authored with AI assistance, identified in its SVG description. It contains no NASA insignia. The audio audition workbench is now a [development archive](dev/audio-audit/README.md), excluded from production.
