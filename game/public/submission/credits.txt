NASA Space Apps 2026: Space Mission Design Game

Design the spacecraft. Research the technology. Secure the hardware. Win support. Can your crew bring the weather images home?

Play: https://missioncontrol.dractec.com/
Source: https://github.com/XDracam/spaceappschallenge2026

Footage: recorded from the current game. This prepared demonstration uses normal game actions, a seeded mission and an accelerated agency calendar, operated by one person/agent across the four station views. The trailer countdown and large captions are editorial. The final flight result is real: two main Weather Satellite goals achieved, zero of two optional goals. This is not a recording of four human players or evidence of learning outcomes.

AI disclosure: The human team created the concept and designed the gameplay. All original project code was written by OpenAI Codex and Anthropic Claude. All project images were generated with ChatGPT Images. Narration was synthesized locally using Voicebox’s Supertonic 3 model (M2). The script, captions and editing automation were AI-generated under human direction.
