What can we use in the game?

All candidates permit free reuse, including commercial use. CC0 needs no attribution. CC BY 3.0 requires creator credit, a source link, a license link, and disclosure of edits. NASA public-domain material has its own source notice. Every candidate includes its specific source and terms; exported picks retain the credits. These assets retain their own terms, separate from the game’s Apache 2.0 license.

Previews are local files. Music previews may be excerpts, so their repeat boundary does not establish whether the full track loops cleanly. Playback levels are adjustable; suitability under player conversation is yours to judge.
