# Mission planner: UX critic review — November 14, 2026

**User direction:** "have critic agents go over all ui elements and score how intuitive they are make improvements on the ones which are confusing/ too complex".

Five critic agents reviewed the redesigned UI in the browser (1440×900) as first-time players. Scores: intuitiveness 1–10 (10 = obvious), complexity 1–10 (10 = too complex). Lowest-scoring elements (intuitiveness ≤ 3) and the fix chosen:

| Area | Element | Int | Cx | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First use | First screen / objective | 2 | 3 | One-time mission briefing card + next-step line driven by plan state |
| Readiness | "Parts not procured" hidden under Show all | 1 | 6 | Always-visible warning "N parts not ordered: launch will be scrubbed" |
| Readiness | Launch now | 2 | 3 | Confirm names date change and undelivered parts; secondary styling |
| Header | Budget chip (showed 0 spent vs 195 M cost) | 3 | 5 | Show estimated cost of budget; signed amount secondary |
| Header | Gear / Dev drawer | 2–3 | 9 | Labelled "Dev", calendar on top, sections collapsible; drawer and panel exclusive (bug: drawer covered panel) |
| Map | Launch-pad pin | 3 | 3 | Empty-plan "Launch from Earth" button; single-option menus act directly |
| Map | Maneuver markers at launch-day position | 4 | 5 | Anchor at the maneuver's own date |
| Map | "+" end-state ring | 4 | 4 | Labelled "+ Next step" pill off the body; overlapping branches spread |
| Map | Aerocapture checkbox | 3 | 2 | Plain label + "Try aerobraking" action on the out-of-fuel error |
| Map | Date line | 3 | 2 | "Planets shown on <date> (launch day / departure / arrival)" |
| Popups | Δv heat map (different number than popup) | 4 | 6 | Same metric labelled; cheap↔expensive legend; best before deadline |

User decisions during the review:

- Versions panel → "Simplify, keep panel (Recommended)".
- "Back to plan" after the playthrough → "No, keep one-shot".
- Procurement panel:

  > the procurement tab is really weird tbh? since we are not working on that players station

  Decision: the planner station has no Procurement panel (consistent with D-006, four players on separate stations). Part status (Draft · Ordered · Contracted · Delivered · Late) and arrival vs launch date live on the Rocket panel's part rows, with a summary line; the readiness warning stays in Issues; the Dev drawer keeps a "Procurement player (stand-in)" section.
- Extra tanks:

  > whats the point of being able to add an extra tank instead of just making it bigger

  The assistant explained two tanks equal one bigger tank (the only real use: adding fuel after a tank is contracted) and proposed keeping only a "top-up tank" for that case. The user answered:

  > Remove completely

  Decision: one tank per stage; the "+ Tank" button and the add-tank action are removed.
