The target experience

Procurement weighs immediate commitments against future needs. Each choice exposes arrival, cost and the consequence for its destination. Players discuss priorities using their own voice chat or conversation.

Decisions this concept proposes

  • Supplier delivery options with visible prices.
  • Delivery amendments with an explicit fee.
  • Call windows of 7, 14 or 30 agency days.
  • Department supply reserved by research projects.

Approved prototype target

Approved November 14, 2026 in D-018. These static studies illustrate the target, not verified gameplay.

The written concept specifies the target behavior, rules, integration files, save migration and observable completion criteria. The screen studies show placement and information hierarchy.

Detailed choices selected here

  • Delivery options change price and arrival. Operational reliability remains a supplier/Resource property.
  • Amendments change delivery on an active Contract and charge an explicit immediate fee.
  • Calls close after 7, 14 or 30 agency days, with visible responder and timing estimates.
  • Science reserves one compatible unit per active project and releases it for reuse when finished.
  • Repeated orders support independent quantities, dates and destinations. The planner combines all contributing Contracts.
  • Split flight orders require identical hardware. A quantity-weighted geometric mean combines supplier reliability, preserving the current per-part game abstraction.
