## Brief summary

Create an interactive game spanning mission design, management, and simulation. Show how engineering choices and limited resources affect mission success. The page discusses objectives, instruments, spacecraft, launch and orbit, budget, mass, power, communications, data, and time.

Solo play and collaboration for up to four participants are suggested. Role responsibilities, scoring, multiple scenarios, and repeated simulations are possibilities rather than a mandatory feature checklist. The Potential Considerations section explicitly presents optional guidance.

## Project interpretation

Procurement, science, scheduling, and external relations are brainstorming ideas for coupled decisions. Spacecraft communications is a separate engineering concern. One-shot simulation, real-time play, and campaign structures are ideas whose relationship is still open.
