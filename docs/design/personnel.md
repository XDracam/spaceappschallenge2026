# Personnel system — brainstorm

Source: [personnel and roles notes](../notes/2026-11-14-personnel-tasks-roles.md). No model has been selected.

## Intent

Explore one consistent personnel system across all roles. Each player could manage their people, while resource procurement (RP) can look for new people. The system should communicate the complexity of training qualified personnel. The user is concerned that without individuals, play could become uninteresting money-allocation sliders.

## Alternatives

| Model | User idea | Questions to explore |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Named individuals | People have names and skills or stats that evolve; they become smarter or better. | How do growth, training, availability, and staffing stay manageable for each role? |
| Skilled pools | Allocate X people in area Y, like power to ship parts in Starfield. | Can training and qualification remain legible and engaging without individual characters? |
| Department headcount | Each department has X people, needs Y, and buys more. | Can a simple count show what makes qualified personnel difficult to train and replace? |

These alternatives can be compared without assuming they have equal appeal or that one has won. Hybrid models are possible but have not been proposed by the user or selected.

## Candidate to explore first — assistant suggestion

Try a small number of named people per role, with a few shared skill and training concepts across roles. This could give staffing a human dimension while making qualification visible. Keep the pool and headcount alternatives available for comparison; the right amount of management and the exact model remain open. No playtest supports this suggestion yet.

## Cross-role interactions to explore

- Science assigns researchers to projects with a duration; projects may also need budget or procurement.
- Procurement can recruit or arrange people; earlier notes suggest astronauts are expensive and hard to replace.
- Every role could manage its own staff under consistent rules, while procurement obtains people.
- Personnel growth, time needed for qualification, assignment conflicts, and loss or replacement are open. The scope of “everyone” and what each role can directly control need definition.
